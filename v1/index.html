02 Design intent

Built around
the air.

R/01 treats speed as an engineering system. Every visible surface answers one of three questions: how cleanly does it arrive, how intelligently does it recover, and how faithfully does it connect?

  1. 01

    Disrupt less

    Reduced frontal obstruction gives the airflow a precise first instruction.

  2. 02

    Recover more

    Hybrid deployment turns deceleration and exhaust energy into repeatable response.

  3. 03

    Control contact

    Thermal discipline keeps each contact patch inside a usable performance window.
